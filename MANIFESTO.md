# Italian Knowledge Graph Manifesto

[English](./MANIFESTO.md) | [Italiano](./docs/it/MANIFESTO.md)

## Why

Knowledge is one of humanity's most valuable assets.

Every day, new knowledge is created, revised and shared. Yet much of it remains fragmented across documents, websites, databases and isolated systems. Even when information is publicly available, it is often difficult to verify, reuse and maintain consistently over time.

The Italian Knowledge Graph exists to help transform knowledge into a structured, collaborative and verifiable public resource.

Its purpose is not simply to model information, but to make knowledge easier to understand, improve and preserve.

---

## Vision

We believe that knowledge should be:

- structured;
- understandable;
- reusable;
- traceable;
- collaborative;
- deterministic;
- independent from any specific AI model or software vendor.

Technology evolves.

Knowledge should remain.

---

## Human expertise comes first

Artificial Intelligence is an extraordinary tool.

It can analyse, suggest, summarise and accelerate many tasks.

However, AI is not the authority.

Within the Italian Knowledge Graph:

- AI may generate;
- AI may propose;
- AI may assist;
- AI may review.

Humans remain responsible for semantic decisions.

Every accepted change should ultimately be explainable and reviewable.

---

## Deterministic governance

Knowledge without governance becomes inconsistent.

Governance without collaboration becomes static.

The Italian Knowledge Graph combines both.

Every contribution follows deterministic validation rules before becoming part of the canonical ontology.

The Validator remains the authoritative reference for structural consistency.

---

## Collaboration

Knowledge grows through collaboration.

The project welcomes contributions from:

- researchers;
- teachers;
- students;
- professionals;
- institutions;
- software developers;
- domain experts;
- enthusiasts.

Contributors should not be required to understand Git, JSON, RDF or graph databases in order to share their expertise.

The project aims to reduce technical barriers while preserving data quality.

---

## Stable knowledge

Identifiers should remain stable.

Changes should be traceable.

Generated artifacts should be reproducible.

Every modification should be understandable by future contributors.

Knowledge should evolve without losing its history.

---

## Openness

The Italian Knowledge Graph is designed to be open.

Its ontology, validation rules and documentation are intended to be transparent and reviewable.

Every design decision should favour clarity over unnecessary complexity.

---

## Quality over quantity

Adding more nodes does not necessarily improve knowledge.

Consistency, clarity and correctness are more important than size.

The objective is not to build the largest ontology.

The objective is to build a trustworthy one.

---

## Independence

The project should remain independent from:

- proprietary AI models;
- cloud services;
- specific database technologies;
- specific user interfaces;
- individual contributors.

Knowledge should outlive the tools used to create it.

---

## Long-term preservation

The Italian Knowledge Graph is designed with long-term sustainability in mind.

Formats may evolve.

Software may evolve.

Artificial Intelligence will evolve.

The knowledge itself should remain accessible, understandable and maintainable.

---

## Our goal

We are not building another ontology editor.

We are building an ecosystem that enables people to collaboratively create, validate and preserve structured knowledge.

The software is a tool.

The knowledge is the purpose.

AI can accelerate knowledge. Only people can take responsibility for it.
