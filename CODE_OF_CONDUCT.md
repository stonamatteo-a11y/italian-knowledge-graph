# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- focus criticism on ideas, data, and implementation;
- provide actionable feedback;
- acknowledge uncertainty and domain limits;
- welcome corrections and differing informed perspectives;
- protect personal and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination;
- personal attacks or inflammatory language;
- deliberate misinformation or repeated bad-faith contributions;
- publishing private information without permission;
- spam, trolling, or disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and comments that violate these standards. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

Concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub or community channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community channels, and public representation of the project.
